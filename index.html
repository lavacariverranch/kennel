Put it on your phone's home screen

  1. In Safari, tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. The brown LRR icon is now on your phone.
  1. In Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add. The brown LRR icon is now on your phone.
  1. Open this page on your phone (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
  2. Use the browser's Add to Home Screen option.

The first time, Google may ask you to sign in. Use your kennel Google account.
